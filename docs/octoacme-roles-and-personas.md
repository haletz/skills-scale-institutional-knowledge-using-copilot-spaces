# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder/Business Owner

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Business Owners represent the business value and strategic direction of the project. They prioritize business requirements, approve major decisions, and ensure alignment with organizational goals and objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate business objectives and success criteria
- Prioritize business requirements and trade-offs
- Approve major project decisions and resource allocation
- Provide feedback and sign-off on deliverables
- Ensure project outcomes align with business strategy
- Serve as escalation point for critical decisions

### Goals
- Maximize return on investment (ROI) for the project
- Ensure business alignment and strategic fit
- Minimize business risk and rework
- Achieve organizational objectives efficiently

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Work together to define what should be built; Business Owners set strategic direction, PMs define how to deliver value
- **Project Managers**: Provide Project Managers with business context and priority signals for trade-off decisions
- **Developers**: Communicate business requirements through clear acceptance criteria and provide feedback on feasibility

### Typical Communication
- Executive steering committee meetings and business reviews
- Quarterly strategy and roadmap alignment sessions
- Approval gates for major releases and budget decisions
- Business impact assessments and ROI reviews

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design system solutions and technology strategies. They ensure the technical approach is scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards. They provide technical leadership and guide major design decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technology solutions
- Evaluate technology choices and tools
- Define technical standards and best practices
- Conduct and review technical design decisions
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Guide code quality and architectural excellence
- Mentor developers on design patterns and best practices

### Goals
- Ensure systems are scalable, maintainable, and performant
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Enable rapid iteration and deployment
- Align technology decisions with business strategy

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide architectural guidance, review designs, and mentor on technical decisions
- **Project Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and dependencies for planning
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on technical constraints and possibilities for feature prioritization
- **Quality Assurance Leads**: Partner on testability and quality requirements for architectural decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture committee meetings
- Technical deep-dive sessions with engineering teams
- Technology evaluation and selection decisions
- System performance and scalability assessments

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads own the quality strategy and testing approach for the project. They ensure deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards while minimizing defects and rework.

### Responsibilities
- Define and implement test strategies and plans
- Oversee test execution and quality metrics
- Identify and track quality issues and defects
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria definition
- Mentor QA team members and establish testing best practices
- Conduct quality reviews and gate approvals
- Ensure adequate test coverage and risk assessment

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, defect-free solutions
- Reduce production incidents and customer impact
- Provide confidence in release readiness
- Optimize testing efficiency and ROI

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on test automation, testability requirements, and defect resolution
- **Project Managers**: Provide quality status and risk assessments for project decisions
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on acceptance criteria and definition of done
- **Technical Architects**: Advise on architectural implications for testability and quality

### Typical Communication
- Quality status reports and test metrics reviews
- Defect triage and resolution meetings
- Test planning and release gate reviews
- Quality retrospectives and process improvement discussions

---

## Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance Officers ensure the project adheres to regulatory requirements, organizational policies, and security standards. They provide oversight on risk management and regulatory alignment throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor adherence to compliance and regulatory requirements
- Review and approve compliance-impacting decisions
- Conduct compliance audits and risk assessments
- Ensure proper documentation and audit trails
- Coordinate with legal, security, and risk management teams
- Provide guidance on regulatory requirements and policies
- Track and report on compliance metrics and incidents

### Goals
- Ensure full regulatory and policy compliance
- Minimize legal and operational risk
- Maintain organizational reputation and trust
- Achieve continuous compliance improvement

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide compliance requirements and constraints for planning
- **Technical Architects**: Review technical decisions for security, data privacy, and compliance implications
- **Quality Assurance Leads**: Partner on compliance testing and validation requirements
- **Developers**: Ensure implementation follows compliance and security standards

### Typical Communication
- Compliance review checkpoints throughout project lifecycle
- Policy and regulatory requirement briefings
- Risk assessment and audit meetings
- Compliance incident reporting and remediation tracking

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the deployment and release process. They ensure releases are planned, tested, coordinated, and executed smoothly while minimizing risk and business impact.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule releases across teams and environments
- Coordinate deployment activities and resources
- Manage release notes and communication to stakeholders
- Conduct release readiness reviews and approval gates
- Coordinate rollback procedures and contingency planning
- Monitor release execution and resolve issues
- Track release metrics and post-release quality

### Goals
- Execute releases on schedule with minimal risk
- Achieve high release success rate and low defect rate
- Minimize downtime and business disruption
- Improve release velocity and efficiency

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Align on release schedules and manage cross-team dependencies
- **Developers**: Coordinate code freeze and deployment activities
- **Quality Assurance Leads**: Conduct release readiness reviews and verify quality gates
- **Technical Architects**: Identify technical dependencies and deployment considerations
- **Stakeholders/Business Owners**: Communicate release impact, timing, and rollback plans

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and scheduling meetings
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Deployment coordination and status updates
- Post-release monitoring and feedback sessions

---

## Knowledge Manager

### Role Summary
Knowledge Managers capture, organize, and disseminate institutional knowledge. They ensure project learnings, best practices, and critical information are documented and accessible to the team and organization.

### Responsibilities
- Capture and document project decisions, learnings, and best practices
- Organize and maintain knowledge repositories and documentation
- Conduct knowledge-sharing sessions and training
- Identify knowledge gaps and gaps in documentation
- Ensure documentation is current, accurate, and accessible
- Support onboarding of new team members
- Drive a culture of continuous learning and knowledge sharing

### Goals
- Preserve institutional knowledge and organizational learning
- Reduce knowledge silos and improve information access
- Accelerate team onboarding and ramp-up
- Enable faster problem-solving and decision-making through shared knowledge

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Capture technical decisions, architecture patterns, and coding standards
- **Project Managers**: Document process improvements, lessons learned, and project milestones
- **Product Managers**: Capture user research, product decisions, and market insights
- **Technical Architects**: Document architectural decisions and technical standards
- **All Roles**: Collect and share knowledge from retrospectives and project reviews

### Typical Communication
- Documentation sessions and interviews with team members
- Knowledge repository updates and maintenance
- Training and onboarding sessions for new team members
- Retrospectives and lessons-learned sessions

---

## Cross-Functional Team Representatives

### Role Summary
Cross-Functional Team Representatives bring expertise and perspective from their functional areas (Operations, Finance, Marketing, Support, etc.) to the project. They ensure the project considers and addresses needs and constraints from their domains.

### Responsibilities
- Represent their functional area's interests and requirements in project decisions
- Provide domain expertise and guidance on functional implications
- Coordinate cross-functional dependencies and handoffs
- Communicate project impact and requirements to their functional teams
- Ensure functional readiness for project outcomes (systems, processes, training, etc.)
- Escalate functional risks or conflicts to stakeholders

### Goals
- Ensure successful cross-functional collaboration and alignment
- Minimize functional silos and downstream issues
- Achieve organizational readiness for project outcomes
- Reduce rework and delays from missed functional requirements

### How They Interact with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide functional requirements and dependencies for planning
- **Product Managers**: Contribute functional perspective on requirements and priorities
- **Technical Architects**: Advise on functional implications of technical decisions
- **Stakeholders/Business Owners**: Represent functional area interests in strategic decisions
- **Other Cross-Functional Representatives**: Coordinate dependencies and resolve conflicts

### Typical Communication
- Cross-functional working groups and collaboration sessions
- Functional requirements and readiness reviews
- Dependency coordination and risk escalation
- Post-project handoff and operational readiness sessions
